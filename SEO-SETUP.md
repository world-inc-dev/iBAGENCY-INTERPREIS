# iBAGENCY Website Launch & Google Search Checklist

Live site: https://world-inc-dev.github.io/iBAGENCY-INTERPREIS/

## Already in the repository
- `robots.txt` allows search crawlers and points to the sitemap.
- `sitemap.xml` lists the canonical homepage.
- The homepage includes a canonical URL, social sharing metadata and organization/FAQ structured data.
- `404.html` provides a branded not-found page.
- Contact form enquiries are prepared in the visitor's browser and handed off to WhatsApp; the website does not store submissions.

## Google Search Console setup (account owner action required)
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console/ and sign in with the Google account that should manage the site.
2. Choose **URL prefix** and enter exactly:
   `https://world-inc-dev.github.io/iBAGENCY-INTERPREIS/`
3. Complete one of the verification methods Google offers. If HTML-file or DNS verification is not practical for a GitHub Pages subpath, use a method Google explicitly offers for this property; do not claim verification until Search Console confirms it.
4. In the verified property, open **Sitemaps** and submit:
   `sitemap.xml`
5. Use **URL Inspection** to request indexing of the homepage if the option is available.
6. Revisit the Performance and Pages reports over the following weeks. Indexing and rankings are not guaranteed and can take time.

## Before promoting the website
- Open the site on a phone and test every navigation link.
- Submit a sample enquiry and check that WhatsApp opens with the correct service, name and phone.
- Confirm the WhatsApp number and all service details are correct.
- Replace illustrative gallery photography with genuine, authorised listing images when available. Do not present sample images as confirmed listings.
- Add individual pages for actual properties only when verified details, permission and useful unique content are available.
- Keep pricing, availability, legal terms and investment risk disclosures accurate. Never promise guaranteed returns.

## Important
The site is a static GitHub Pages website. Its current contact form does not send or store enquiries on a server; the visitor must review and send the prepared message in WhatsApp.
